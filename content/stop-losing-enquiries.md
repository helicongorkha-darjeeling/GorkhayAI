Ask any hotel owner in Darjeeling where their bookings come from and you'll hear the same list: walk-ins, phone calls, WhatsApp, a few OTAs, and "people who found us on Google".

Now ask where the *enquiries that didn't book* went. Usually nobody knows.

That gap is where most small properties quietly lose money. Not to a better hotel down the road, but to a phone that rang during check-out, a WhatsApp message that scrolled out of view, or a website form that emailed an inbox nobody opens.

## The three things every enquiry needs

A guest enquiry is only useful if three things happen:

1. **It gets captured.** Name, phone number and dates, saved somewhere permanent, not in someone's head.
2. **It gets seen.** By the person at the front desk, on the screen they already use.
3. **It gets a call back.** Fast, and marked as done so it isn't called twice or forgotten.

## What we set up

For Hotel Teesta we built exactly that, and nothing more complicated than it needs to be:

- **The website asks for a phone number early.** As soon as a guest starts looking at dates, we save their details, even if they never finish booking.
- **Every enquiry is stored in a database.** We use Supabase, which is secure, inexpensive and easy to export from.
- **The front desk sees a simple sheet.** Each row shows the guest, their dates, and how far they got: *details, rooms, review, payment, booked*.
- **Staff tick it off after calling.** That one checkbox is what turns a list of names into a habit.

> The best lead system is the one your front desk actually uses. Start with a sheet, not a dashboard.

## Why "how far they got" matters

Someone who only typed their phone number is a different call from someone who reached the payment step and stopped. The second guest almost certainly wants the room. A friendly call ("Did something go wrong with the payment? I can hold the room for you") often saves the booking.

## Start small

You don't need an AI bot on day one. You need every enquiry written down and seen by a human. Once that is working, a booking bot can answer the easy questions at 2 a.m. and hand the warm leads to your team in the morning.
